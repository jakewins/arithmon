## Pizel.ttf

["Pizel"](https://copheemoth.itch.io/pizel) by
[CopheeMoth](https://copheemoth.itch.io/), licensed under
[CC-0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Vendored
verbatim from upstream Tuxemon's `thin_font_file`
(`upstream/mods/tuxemon/font/Pizel.ttf`). Used for `SMALL` / `SMALL_HEADING`
UI text (combat prompt, HUD names, monster-info rows) where PressStart2P's
8-px-native outlines don't grid-align at 5-7 px (STORY-0229).
